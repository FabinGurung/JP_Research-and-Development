This is a separate versioned work branch. It preserves the v0.4 work branch and does **not** modify the original company letterhead or logo Google Docs.

`templates/enterprise/v2/client-contract-simple-lumpsum-letterhead.tex`.

## Document layout
- A4 page, left/right margins **24 mm**, top/bottom margins **25 mm**.
- Header has company name, address, PAN, registration number, reference and date; **no corporate logo at top**.
- The logo is placed **once**, inside `\ContractPartySignatures{OWNER}`, above the contractor signature and date lines on the **final page**.
- To use this for other companies, resolve `company-brand.tex` and the approved logo file from the owning company’s private Drive references. Do not commit private assets or contract text to GitHub.
- The v0.5 branded Radha Roka contract remains an **unsigned draft**, maintaining NPR 57,50,000 and the complete scope from v0.4.
