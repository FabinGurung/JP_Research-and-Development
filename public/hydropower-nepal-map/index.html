Geometry + query rule

Projects = points · rivers = lines · basins/catchments = polygons. Web geometry is EPSG:4326. Metric distance/area queries should use PostGIS geography or an appropriate verified projected CRS, not raw degree distances.

Source distinction

The live public river lines are HydroRIVERS-derived and the four major basin polygons are HydroBASINS-derived. Nepal government Survey Department and WECS sources remain the national authority context; these public vectors are not mislabeled as official Nepal-government vector files.
