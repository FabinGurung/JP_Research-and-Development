Important naming correction

Kali Gandaki ≠ a single project location.
Kali Gandaki is the river/geographic name. Kali Gandaki A is a hydropower project/station. Its highlighted map point is the powerhouse/generating-station coordinate near Beltari/Yamgha, while its dam/headworks are upstream near Mirmi.

Ranking rule

“Top 10” means ten major operating hydropower projects verified for this map and ordered by installed/nameplate capacity. It does not rank present-day output, annual energy, reliability, economics or environmental performance.
