\chapter*{UNITS AND CONVERSIONS}
\addcontentsline{toc}{chapter}{UNITS AND CONVERSIONS}
The International System of Units (SI) is used where appropriate. Length is expressed in millimetres (mm) or metres (m); area in square metres (m$^2$); volume in cubic metres (m$^3$); mass in kilograms (kg); and duration in hours (h) or days (d). Monetary figures, where supported by source records, will state the applicable currency, quantities and recorded rates.
