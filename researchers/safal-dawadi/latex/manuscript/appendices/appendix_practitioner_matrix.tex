\PUAddAppendixEntry{FIELD OBSERVATION SUMMARY}
\label{app:practitioner}
I prepared this summary from my site notes and construction observations. It groups the workmanship concerns that I considered most relevant to waterproofing, concrete paving and masonry/plaster work. The original Nepali notes are preserved separately. The site record and applicable drawing should be examined before concluding that a particular condition caused failure or required corrective work.
