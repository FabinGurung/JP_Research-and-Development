\chapter{SITE OBSERVATION AND REWORK CASE RECORDING FORM}
\PUAddAppendixEntry{SITE OBSERVATION AND REWORK CASE RECORDING FORM}
\label{app:observation}
The same recording structure should be applied to each possible rework event in W01 waterproofing, W02 project-associated PCC pavement, or W03 plaster/masonry. Entries should distinguish an observed symptom from a verified mechanism, cause, corrective intervention and quantified consequence. No blank entry should be interpreted as a negative finding.
