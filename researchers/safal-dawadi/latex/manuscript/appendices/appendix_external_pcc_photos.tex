\chapter{COMPARATIVE PHOTOGRAPHIC RECORD: EXTERNAL PCC ROAD WORK}
\label{app:safal-road-pcc}
These eight road-PCC photographs are retained solely as a visual technical comparison. The researcher subsequently described the road work as public-road or separately contracted infrastructure, and reported visible cracks without subsequent replacement or repair; other images portray preventive joint-like construction. The PCC examples are thus not admitted as RCC-building-project rework cases. No frequency, cost, delay or demonstrated causal ranking has been inferred. The reported concerns regarding curing require dated project inspection or test evidence before being treated as verified causes.
