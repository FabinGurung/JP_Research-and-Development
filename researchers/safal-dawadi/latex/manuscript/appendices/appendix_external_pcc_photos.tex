\chapter{COMPARATIVE PHOTOGRAPHIC RECORD: EXTERNAL PCC ROAD WORK}
\label{app:safal-road-pcc}
I examined eight photographs from separate road-concreting work to compare visible cracking with construction-joint arrangements. The photographed pavement work was reported as independent road or infrastructure construction rather than an established component of the sampled residential-building work. At the photographed cracked locations, I did not establish any later removal or repair. I have therefore used these images only to explain the field conditions and construction practices, not as completed RCC-building rework cases. No frequency, cost, delay or demonstrated causal ranking has been inferred. The site reports about curing were not supported by a recorded curing duration or test result for the particular photographed cracks, so I have not assigned curing as their proven cause.
