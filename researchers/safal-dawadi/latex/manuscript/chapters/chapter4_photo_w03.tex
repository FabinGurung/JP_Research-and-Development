\subsection{Photographic review of brick walls and construction junctions}
I supervised masonry work during construction and observed concerns involving wall junctions and workmanship at different locations. I also reported a separate instance of masonry demolition and reconstruction. The five photographs presented here show masonry stages and interfaces; they have not yet been matched to the full before-and-after record of that particular reconstruction event. I therefore do not assign its additional cost or duration from these images alone. A marked exterior bay does not independently establish absence of concealed sill or lintel reinforcement. Drawings and inspection records govern any technical compliance claim.
