# Rupesh Man Pradhan GIS V03.0 — All wards colored

**Only cartographic change from V02.7:** all 33 ward polygons have individual fill colors and corresponding legend entries. Previously, the eleven 'OK' wards were colored and the other 22 were grey.

**Unchanged:** the 11 OK ward colors, all 95 individual building markers (50 engineered, 45 non-engineered), exclusion of non-OK-ward building records, 9 display-geometry holds, original research data, ward polygons, national locator, 5 km buffered square extent, north arrow, graticule, UTM Zone 45N projection, and 5 km scale bar.
