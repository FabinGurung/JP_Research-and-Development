\justifying
\PUAbstractParagraph{This thesis assesses the seismic performance of low-rise reinforced concrete buildings in Nepal and the practical applicability limits of NBC 205 Ready-to-Use Detailing (RUD). A representative Draft Final NBC 205:2012 building is examined through its available concrete design-check results. The main analytical comparison comprises the NBC 205:2024 Regular building and six plan-irregular configurations (L, L1, L2, T, U and Non-Parallel), each with three full storeys plus a stair cover. A separate Regular model with four full storeys plus a stair cover is considered as a height extension, but is excluded from the matched seven-model performance-point comparison.}
\PUAbstractParagraph{Using the NBC 105:2025 demand cases for Soil Types C and D and the selected seismic zone factors, the study compares modal periods and participating mass, storey shear, inter-storey drift, centre-of-mass--centre-of-rigidity (CM--CR) separation, nonlinear pushover capacity, performance points, available final-step hinge observations and concrete design checks. For Soil Type C with $Z=0.40$, fundamental periods of the seven-model set range from 0.649 to 0.707~s. Mode~1 contributes approximately 79.09--80.32\% of translational mass in X and Mode~2 approximately 74.82--80.42\% in Y; cumulative participation exceeds 90\% by Mode~4 in X and Mode~5 in Y for every configuration.}
\PUAbstractParagraph{In the Soil C, $Z=0.40$ comparison, the Regular model has a validated Story-1 EQx storey shear of 532.489~kN and the largest performance-point base shear of 456.253~kN (X direction). The U-shaped model has the largest governing ULS drift ratio of 0.004954, below the adopted 0.025 limit, the largest first-peak pushover base shear of 1882.070~kN (Y direction) and the largest performance-point roof displacement of 27.072~mm (X direction). The maximum reported main-storey CM--CR separation is approximately 1.237~m for the Non-Parallel model at Story~2 under Soil C, $Z=0.25$; this separation is not a formal torsional-irregularity classification. The historical 2012 reference has 1 failing concrete-design-check frame at Soil C, $Z=0.25$ and 21 at Soil C, $Z=0.35$ and $0.40$.}
\PUAbstractParagraph{The models do not exhibit a unique or monotonic seismic-response ordering by study-defined Geometric Irregularity Index (GII). Different configurations control different force, deformation and capacity measures. Although individual response checks can fall within adopted limits, these results do not establish unrestricted RUD suitability for irregular plans. Project-specific seismic verification is required, and the available evidence does not support a quantitative four-storey height-effect comparison.}
