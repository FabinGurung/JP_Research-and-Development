The plotted responses therefore do not support treating GII as a unique predictor of seismic behaviour.

Figure~\ref{fig:gii_multiresponse_cp112b} further shows the non-unique relationship between GII and performance-point base shear. Models sharing the same GII remain separated in response, indicating that plan topology and stiffness distribution contribute information that is not contained in the area-deficit index alone.
