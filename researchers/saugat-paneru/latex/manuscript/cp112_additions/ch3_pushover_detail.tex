
\subsection{Pushover and Hinge-Modelling Procedure}
The available nonlinear-analysis results contain separate X- and Y-direction pushover curves and embedded performance-point outputs for all seven models. These establish the analysis directions and the response quantities used in this thesis. The available final-step hinge image set also establishes that hinge formation was reviewed in both pushover directions for the five matched demand cases.

The available analysis records do not consistently document the setup fields requested for a fully reproducible nonlinear-analysis definition---specifically the exact control-node identifier, prescribed control displacement/target displacement, total step definition, gravity-to-pushover sequence, and member-by-member hinge-assignment metadata. The retained ETABS model definitions record \texttt{PDELTA METHOD ``NONE''}, but the thesis does not generalize that setting beyond those model definitions. Similarly, exact beam/column hinge types and P--M2--M3 assignment locations are not reconstructed from screenshots. These undocumented setup details are therefore identified as a study limitation rather than inferred.

For interpretation, capacity curves are compared to the first global peak, performance-point shear/displacement are kept separate from peak capacity, and final-step hinge screenshots are not treated as performance-point hinge states unless directly solved at that state.
