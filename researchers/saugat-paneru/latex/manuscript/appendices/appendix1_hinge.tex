\section*{General}

The appendix presents the complete final-step hinge-formation image set supplied for the seven NBC 205:2024 three-storey-plus-stair-cover building configurations. Each image combines the hinge state recorded at the final available analysis step for the X- and Y-direction pushover analyses for one soil/seismic-demand case. These final-step images are not interpreted as performance-point hinge states unless a hinge state is directly available from a solved performance-point output. The complete image set is retained at readable scale, with two hinge-formation figures arranged per page wherever space permits to reduce unnecessary blank areas while preserving member and hinge-state visibility.

The five demand cases are Soil Type C with $Z=0.25$, $0.30$, $0.35$, and $0.40$, and Soil Type D with $Z=0.35$. These appendix figures support the nonlinear-response discussion in Chapter 4 and are kept separate from the distinct four-storey observed-failure evidence.
