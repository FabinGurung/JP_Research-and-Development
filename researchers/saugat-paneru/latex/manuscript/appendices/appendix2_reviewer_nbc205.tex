\section*{Source and permitted interpretation}
Extracts below reproduce the relevant plan-configuration and RC reinforcement/detailing illustrations in the identified archival source, \emph{Draft Final NBC 205:2012, Ready to Use Guideline for Detailings of Low Rise Reinforced Concrete Buildings without Masonry Infill}, Government of Nepal, Department of Urban Development and Building Construction (Drive-verified original). Page references are original PDF page indices. These are historical prescriptive reference illustrations, not the final revised NBC 205:2024 provisions and not evidence of the actual analysed ETABS reinforcement. Building-specific verification remains separately necessary.
