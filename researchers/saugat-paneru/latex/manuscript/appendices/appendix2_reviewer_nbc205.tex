\section*{NBC 205:2012 detailing provisions}
Extracts below reproduce the relevant plan-configuration and RC reinforcement/detailing illustrations in the identified archival source, \emph{NBC 205:2012, Ready to Use Guideline for Detailing of Low-Rise Reinforced Concrete Buildings without Masonry Infill}, Government of Nepal, Department of Urban Development and Building Construction. Page references are original PDF page indices. These are historical prescriptive reference illustrations, not the final revised NBC 205:2024 provisions and not evidence of the actual analysed ETABS reinforcement. Building-specific verification remains separately necessary.
