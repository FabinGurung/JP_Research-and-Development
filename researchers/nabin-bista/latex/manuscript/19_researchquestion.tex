\section{Research Questions}
This study is guided by the following research questions:
\begin{enumerate}
    \item How does soil--structure interaction affect the modal characteristics of the selected mid-size general hospital building?
    \item How do fixed-base and flexible-base models compare in terms of response-spectrum displacement, inter-storey drift, base shear, and storey shear?
    \item How do foundation settlement and support reaction response vary across the four model conditions?
    \item How do beam and column force envelopes vary with increasing foundation flexibility?
    \item How does soil flexibility affect nonlinear pushover capacity and the reported FEMA~356 performance-point coordinates in the two principal directions?
    \item What preliminary fragility trends are obtained from the available twelve-ground-motion nonlinear time-history results and the adopted drift-based damage states?
\end{enumerate}
