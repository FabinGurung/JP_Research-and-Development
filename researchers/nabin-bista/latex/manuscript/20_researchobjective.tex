\section{Research Objectives}
The general objective of this study is to assess the influence of soil--structure interaction on the seismic performance and preliminary fragility of a mid-size general hospital building in Nepal.

The specific objectives are:
\begin{enumerate}
    \item To develop and compare fixed-base, hard-soil, medium-soil, and soft-soil analytical models of the selected hospital building.
    \item To evaluate modal characteristics and response-spectrum seismic demands, including displacement, drift, base shear, and storey shear.
    \item To compare foundation settlement, support reaction, beam-force, and column-force responses across the four support conditions.
    \item To evaluate nonlinear static pushover capacity and compare the reported performance-point coordinates of the fixed and flexible support conditions using the FEMA~356 target-displacement framework.
    \item To develop and interpret preliminary fragility relationships from the nonlinear response of twelve ground motions using the adopted damage-state thresholds.
\end{enumerate}
