\begin{thesisabstract}
\phantomsection\label{front:abstract}
\abstractparagraph{This study examines soil--structure interaction in six reinforced-concrete hospital blocks under fixed-base, hard-, medium- and soft-soil support. The analysis compares modal response, seismic deformation, shear, foundation behaviour and fragility for the G+4 hospital in Pokhara.}

\abstractparagraph{From fixed to soft support, fundamental periods increased by 5.22--19.73\%, while at least 90\% cumulative modal mass participation in both horizontal directions was reached by Modes 5--10. X-direction uppermost-storey displacement increased with foundation flexibility in all six blocks; the maximum reported inter-storey drift was 0.774\% in Block 3 under soft-soil support. Base-shear changes varied among blocks and did not consistently decrease as the support softened. Under the separate $1.2DL+1.5LL$ gravity-load combination, Block 1 foundation settlement increased from 0.592~mm on hard soil to 9.925~mm on soft soil.}

\abstractparagraph{Twelve ground-motion records informed the nonlinear-response and fragility dataset. Its mean operational damage-threshold intensity was 0.142~$g$ for fixed support and 0.088~$g$ for soft support; other tabulated damage-state thresholds showed the same directional difference. The X-direction fragility curves provide a further comparison across foundation conditions. Together, the findings demonstrate the need to assess vibration period, deformation, shear, foundation movement and damage exceedance jointly.}

\abstractkeywords{Soil--Structure Interaction; Hospital Buildings; Seismic Response; Ground Motions; Seismic Fragility.}
\end{thesisabstract}
