\begin{thesisabstract}
\phantomsection\label{front:abstract}
\abstractparagraph{This study evaluates soil--structure interaction in six structural blocks of a G+4 hospital in Pokhara. Three-dimensional ETABS models were compared under fixed-base, hard-, medium- and soft-soil support using modal and NBC 105:2025 response-spectrum analyses. Foundation settlement and member forces were assessed separately under $1.2DL+1.5LL$. Twelve ground-motion records were used in the associated fragility-threshold dataset.}
\abstractparagraph{The fundamental periods increased by 5.22--19.73\% from fixed to soft support among the six blocks. At least 90\% cumulative participating mass was obtained in both principal horizontal directions. Block~1 X-direction uppermost-storey displacement increased from 59.39 to 110.92~mm (86.8\%), while the maximum reported inter-storey drift was 0.774\% in Block~3 under soft support. Base-shear results remained nearly constant across the support cases in several blocks, whereas maximum storey shear varied. Under the gravity combination, Block~1 foundation settlement increased from 0.592~mm on hard soil to 9.925~mm on soft soil.}
\abstractparagraph{For the twelve-record dataset, the mean operational damage-threshold intensity decreased from 0.142~$g$ under fixed support to 0.088~$g$ under soft support. The study also presents X-direction fragility illustrations for the six blocks. The findings show that the effects of soil flexibility differ between vibration, deformation, forces, foundation movement and damage-state exceedance, and require assessment through several response measures.}
\abstractkeywords{Soil--Structure Interaction; Hospital Buildings; Response-Spectrum Analysis; Foundation Settlement; Seismic Fragility.}
\end{thesisabstract}
