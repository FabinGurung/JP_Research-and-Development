\section{Research Questions}
\begin{enumerate}
\item How does soil--structure interaction influence the dynamic characteristics (natural period, frequency and mode shapes) of district hospital buildings under fixed-base and flexible-base conditions for hard, medium and soft soil profiles?
\item How does soil flexibility affect the seismic response of district hospital buildings in terms of storey displacement, inter-storey drift, base shear, storey shear and beam and column force demands?
\item How does soil--structure interaction influence foundation behaviour, particularly foundation settlement and vertical reactions, under different soil conditions?
\item How does soil--structure interaction influence the seismic fragility of district hospital buildings under different soil conditions?
\end{enumerate}
