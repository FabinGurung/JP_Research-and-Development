\section{Research Objectives}
\subsection*{General Objective}
To evaluate the seismic performance and fragility of district hospital buildings in Nepal by investigating the influence of soil--structure interaction under different soil conditions.

\subsection*{Specific Objectives}
\begin{enumerate}
\item To investigate the influence of soil--structure interaction on the dynamic characteristics of district hospital buildings under fixed-base and flexible-base conditions for hard, medium and soft soil profiles.
\item To evaluate the effects of soil flexibility on the seismic response of hospital buildings in terms of storey displacement, inter-storey drift, base shear, storey shear and structural member-force demands using response-spectrum analysis.
\item To assess the influence of soil--structure interaction on foundation behaviour by evaluating foundation settlement and vertical reactions under different soil conditions.
\item To evaluate the influence of soil--structure interaction on the seismic fragility of district hospital buildings through fragility assessment in the X-direction.
\end{enumerate}
