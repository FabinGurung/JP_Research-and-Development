\section{Research Objectives}
\subsection*{General Objective}
To evaluate the seismic performance and fragility of the six-block hospital case study in Pokhara by investigating soil--structure interaction under fixed-base and hard-, medium- and soft-soil support conditions.

\subsection*{Specific Objectives}
\begin{enumerate}
\item To investigate the influence of soil--structure interaction on the dynamic characteristics of hospital buildings under fixed-base and flexible-base conditions representing hard, medium and soft soil profiles.
\item To evaluate the effects of soil flexibility on seismic storey displacement, inter-storey drift, base shear, storey shear and member-force demand through response-spectrum analysis, together with foundation settlement and vertical reactions for the specified gravity-load combination.
\item To assess the influence of soil--structure interaction on the X-direction seismic fragility of the hospital blocks by comparing response and damage-state exceedance across the considered foundation-support conditions.
\end{enumerate}
