Method statement

Six ordered RCC beam-work steps

The export contains the same six-step template for AB and BC (12 rows total). One sequence is shown below with its two-element applicability disclosed.

01

Check drawings and dimensions

Verify beam location, span, breadth, depth, levels, and supporting points using approved drawings and shared project element data.

Inspection
Confirm beam dimensions and coordinates before work starts.
Responsible
Site engineer
02

Prepare and fix formwork

Install beam bottom and side formwork, ensuring proper line, level, support, tight joints, and required dimensions.

Inspection
Check formwork line, level, dimensions, and stability.
Responsible
Site supervisor
03

Place reinforcement

Place bottom bars, top bars, stirrups, extra bars, laps, hooks, and spacers as per structural drawing.

Inspection
Check bar size, spacing, lap length, cover, and anchorage.
Responsible
Site engineer
04

Pre-concreting inspection

Inspect reinforcement, formwork, cover blocks, embedded items, cleanliness, and access before concrete placement.

Inspection
Approve pour card or inspection checklist before concreting.
Responsible
Engineer / QA
05

Concrete placement and compaction

Place concrete continuously in the beam and compact using suitable vibration without displacing reinforcement or formwork.

Inspection
Check concrete workability, placement, and compaction.
Responsible
Site supervisor
06

Curing and protection

Protect the beam from disturbance and begin curing after initial setting as per project specification.

Inspection
Confirm curing method and duration.
Responsible
Site supervisor

Specification

Five RCC beam-work clauses with acceptance criteria

The same five-clause template is linked to both elements (10 rows total).

01Implemented

Materials

Concrete, reinforcement, binding wire, cover blocks, and formwork materials shall conform to approved project requirements.

Acceptance

Materials shall be approved before use.

02Implemented

Formwork

Formwork shall be rigid, properly supported, leak-proof, and set to correct line, level, and dimensions.

Acceptance

No excessive deflection, leakage, or dimensional deviation.

03Implemented

Reinforcement

Reinforcement shall be placed as per approved structural drawings with required cover, spacing, laps, hooks, and anchorage.

Acceptance

Bar diameter, spacing, cover, and placement shall match approved drawings.

04Implemented

Concrete placement

Concrete shall be placed without segregation and compacted properly using appropriate vibration.

Acceptance

Concrete shall be compact, uniform, and free from honeycombing.

05Implemented

Curing

Concrete shall be cured after initial setting and protected from premature drying, impact, or disturbance.

Acceptance

Curing shall be maintained for the required project duration.
